# Dataset audit — items needing independent review

Generated by auditing all 55 datasets against three sources: the RA source
CSV (`final_database.csv`), the RA spreadsheet (`Liking Rating Database.xlsx`,
sheet *final datasets*), and the published papers.


## A. Placeholder item names — `nouniqueitem`

`nouniqueitem` is the RA's marker for "source file had no item labels". It is
currently stored as though it were a real stimulus.


| Dataset | Placeholder ratings | Of dataset total | What it means |
|---|---|---|---|
| `brusaeb` | 33 | 33 (**100%**) | The whole dataset. Paper rated 64 foods; all 1,907 source rows are unlabeled, collapsed to 33 per-subject means over unidentifiable stimuli. |
| `romfred` | 1 | 27,108 (0.004%) | Subject 1095's 66 unlabeled rows collapsed to one value. |

**In the database: 2 datasets.** Eight more carry the same defect in the source
CSV and were correctly excluded from the database (desself, foljac1, kraeglu, kramits, ostroglu, sebmar, shenhav1a, tanhar).


A further three (`hasdes`, `shenhav1b`, `shenhav2`) had one blank-named item each
in the source; the importer dropped it. Correct behaviour, no action.

## C. `romfred` — three separate problems

Study: Frömer, R., Callaway, F., Griffiths, T. L., & Shenhav, A. (2025).
*Open Mind*, 9, 791–813. doi:10.1162/opmi.a.3


1. **Declared scale is wrong.** We report −10..10. The paper states ratings were
   made *"on a scale from 0 (not at all) to 10 (a great deal)"*. The data agrees:
   30,850 source rows, range 0..10, with exactly **one** negative value — which is
   on the `nouniqueitem` placeholder. The `-10_to_10` label came from the RA's
   `rating_scale` column.

2. **Consequence: `normalized_rating` is corrupted.** With min=−10, all 27,108
   normalized values fall in **0.5..1.0** instead of 0..1 — biased upward against
   every other dataset. This is the field required for cross-study comparison.
   *No other dataset in the database has this problem.*

3. **Subject count does not match the paper.** The paper reports 30 + 31 = **61**
   participants across two studies and no third sample. We hold **92** subjects,
   in one contiguous ID block (1001–1095), 189–319 items each. Unexplained.


## D. Scales: database vs. paper

The spreadsheet's `scale` column is unreliable; its `question_asked` column
(prose transcribed from each paper) is the trustworthy field.


| Dataset | We report | Sheet `scale` | Paper says | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `balim` | 1..5 | 1_to_4 | "5-point scale" | **we are right**, sheet wrong |
| `toyam` | 1..8 | 1_to_4 | "eight-point Likert scale" | **we are right**, sheet wrong |
| `romfred` | −10..10 | −10_to_10 | "0 (not at all) to 10" | **we are wrong** |
| `libain1` | 0..100 | 0_to_10 | "a scale from 0 to 10" | data is 0..100 in 0.5 steps; likely slider resolution vs. reported scale. Normalization unaffected. |
| `libain2` | 0..100 | 0_to_10 | "a scale from 0 to 10" | same as libain1 |
| `foljac2` | 0..1 (wtp) | 0_to_3 | "£0 to £3" BDM | arrived pre-normalized; codebook wrongly says **$**0–3 |

## E. `shevsmith2` — do not import

Listed on the RA's final-dataset sheet but never imported. Its 106 item names are
**100% identical to `shevsmith1`'s candy names**, while the sheet's own
`question_asked` says the stimuli were *"107 abstract art images"*. The food key
was joined onto the art ratings.


Resolved: art images are out of scope for this database. Only `shevsmith1`
(the food dataset) belongs. `shevsmith2` should be permanently excluded.
